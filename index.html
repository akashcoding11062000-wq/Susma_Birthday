Happy Birthday, Susma ❤️

A little letter from my heart to yours.

Happy Birthday to my dearest and loveliest Susma, my love. ❤️

I don't even know where to begin, because whenever I think about us, so many memories come rushing back to me that I don't know which one to write first.

Somewhere along the way, I started liking you. The warmth, closeness, care, and affection you gave me slowly made me forget everything else around me. Without even realising it, I started living in a different world where there was mostly just you. You became like the sun, and I became the earth, constantly revolving around you. 🌍☀️❤️

Everything was going so beautifully. I still remember our first kiss. How you rejected me at first, and then, somehow, we kissed. I can still remember the warmth of your lips and the feeling that ran through my entire body at that moment. It is one of those memories that I don't think I will ever forget. ❤️

And then our dance... Maybe it was the first time I had ever danced with someone while holding her so close. Holding you by your back, your waist, hugging you, being so close to you... all those little moments made me feel even more connected to you. I don't know if you realised it then, but every one of those moments made me fall for you a little more.

Then there were those nights when we cried together. Your scolds, your efforts, the way you cared for me, the little things you did for me, the way you were there when I needed you... all of it made me revolve around you even more. I was barely able to focus on anything else because somehow, my mind always came back to you. ❤️

Then you went for your internship, and suddenly I started wondering, "How am I going to survive this last semester without her?" It was hard. Really hard. But somewhere inside, I still knew you were with me. I knew you wouldn't leave me. And sometimes just thinking about that was enough to give me butterflies.

Maybe in seven lifetimes, you would still be the only person whom I could love this much. ❤️

Maybe I didn't fall for your anger or your hard voice. I fell for the softness you carried around me. The way you could make me understand things without shouting, without scolding, without being harsh with me. That softness made me realise that you were different. You were someone I didn't want to lose.

And then came that last day.

I don't even know how much I cried that night. I couldn't even properly show my eyes to my friends the next day. My heart was hurting so badly. That night felt like pins were piercing through my heart again and again. 💔

And then, the next day, when you hugged me, cried and said that we wouldn't stay together... I don't think I can ever explain how painful that moment was.

It felt like someone had taken a knife and pierced straight through my heart.

It was so painful that I wouldn't wish that kind of pain upon anyone. 💔

Still, when I went home, one thought kept coming back to me:

"How can I lose someone whom I love this much?"

That's why I chose to stay with you.

And honestly, these two years have been some of the best days of my life. ❤️

I travelled by airplane for the first time for my loved one—for you. I remember that journey, and I remember so many other little things we've done together. Every place, every conversation, every laugh, every argument, every hug... somehow became a part of my life.

And I have always been proud of you.

I can proudly stand in front of anyone and say that my girlfriend works, works hard, and has even helped me financially when I needed it. I have never felt ashamed of that. Instead, I've always felt proud that the person I love is strong, independent and capable. ❤️

But maybe happiness doesn't come alone.

Sometimes, along with happiness, sorrow comes too.

And I know that recently, so many things have happened between us. Things have changed. Things that were once jokes between us have somehow turned into arguments. We've hurt each other, sometimes intentionally and sometimes without even realising it.

For everything I have done that has hurt you, I am genuinely sorry. ❤️

Right now, I don't want more arguments. I don't want more negativity between us. I just need a little break. I want both of us to breathe, calm down, understand each other and, most importantly, start fresh.

I want us to become the way we were earlier.

The two people who could laugh at the smallest things.
The two people who could turn even ordinary moments into beautiful memories.
The two people who cared for each other without keeping score.
The two people who could be happy simply because they had each other. ❤️

Maybe after this break, some of these things won't happen anymore. Maybe we'll understand each other better. Maybe we'll learn how to love each other without hurting each other.

I don't know exactly what the future holds for us.

But I know that these two years with you have meant more to me than I can ever properly put into words.

I've ordered a few small gifts for you. They aren't anything expensive or huge—just little things that I can afford right now. But I chose them with you in my mind, and I hope when you see them, you remember that someone was thinking about you while choosing them. ❤️🎁

Today is your birthday, and more than anything else, I want you to be happy.

I want you to achieve everything you dream about. I want to see you succeed, grow, smile and live the life you deserve.

And somewhere in between all these wishes, I have one small wish for us too...

I hope you can forgive me.

I hope we can leave behind some of the pain we've caused each other.

I hope we can start again.

I pray that you get into Qualcomm and achieve everything you've worked so hard for. ❤️

And I hope that someday, somehow, we can be together again—roaming the streets just like we used to during our B.Tech days, walking around without caring about the time, laughing at the smallest things, making silly memories and just being happy because we had each other. 🥹❤️

Maybe that's all I want—to see you achieve your dreams and, at the same time, have us find our way back to the happiness we once had.

And I hope, someday, when we look back at these difficult days, we'll realise that they were just a chapter in our story—not the ending. ❤️

Happy Birthday, my dearest and loveliest Susma. ❤️🎂
Thank you for coming into my life.
Thank you for all the love and care.
Thank you for every laugh.
Thank you for every hug.
Thank you for every tear.
Thank you for every effort.
Thank you for staying with me through all these years.

Maybe I don't always know how to express what I feel, and maybe I've made mistakes along the way.

But one thing I know for sure...
I have loved you deeply. ❤️

And I will always be grateful for the beautiful memories we've created together.

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️
May this year bring you happiness, success, peace and everything your heart wishes for.

May you forgive me. ❤️

Happy Birthday, Susma.
My dearest. My loveliest. My love. 🫶🏻❤️
